# push-to-brain dry run, 2026-09-22

The seven test cases the skill's brief asked for, run against a fixture vault built in the session
scratchpad (a Tenders project, one decided decision, an area, a stub domain, a Decision template,
and a Personal page seeded with the same words as the work pages so the wall could be tested). The
helper `scripts/brain-locate.mjs` did the locating, the same-fact checks and the birth gate; the
edits were made the way the skill's `change-patterns.md` says to make them. Each case records what
the helper returned and what changed.

The helper was also exercised read-only against the live vault first: `find storefront websites`
returned the project README, its research pages and the VPC decision record, ranked in that order;
`check` on a live decision page came back clean with four inbound links; `find golf health finance`
returned five work pages and nothing from the Personal layers. Each command runs in under two
seconds once the disk cache is warm. The first call on a cold cache took longer than two minutes on
the eleven-thousand-entry vault, which is the disk, not the script.

## What the run showed about the design

- **The order holds.** Cases 1 to 3 landed on the existing README by append, edit-with-history and
  insert without a new file. Case 6 was the only creation, and only after the search returned
  nothing above the ledger.
- **The birth gate does its job.** Case 6 was refused until it had an inbound link, which is the
  rule Brain Maintenance §6 added after eighteen orphan pages arrived in ten days.
- **Idempotency is the same-fact check plus dates.** Cases 4 and 7 wrote nothing because every line
  the skill writes carries its date and its phrase, so the next run can find it.
- **The wall is enforced by the helper, not by discipline.** The Personal page shared every key
  term with the work pages and never appeared, because the walker excludes those paths before
  scoring.
- **Not tested here:** the harvest step itself (reading a real conversation and marking items) is
  the model's judgement and has no fixture. The seven cases test everything downstream of it.
