# Voice

The house style already exists in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) and every skill follows it. This
page is that rule made explicit, with the reasoning and some worked examples.

## The rules

1. **British English.** Behaviour, organise, prioritise, whilst is still not a word we use.
2. **No em dashes.** A comma, a full stop, or a colon. The em dash is the punctuation mark of
   text that was generated rather than written, and this brand cannot afford to read that way.
3. **No buzzwords.** Not leverage, not seamless, not empower, not unlock, not supercharge, not
   "AI-powered". If a sentence survives deleting the adjective, delete it.
4. **Short declarative sentences.** Say the thing. Then say the next thing.
5. **Concrete nouns and real numbers.** "Thirty-two skills" beats "a comprehensive suite".
6. **Never claim what has not been measured.** The brand's whole credibility rests on the
   counts being generated rather than typed.
7. **Second person for instructions, first person plural for beliefs.** "Add it to the plugin's
   skills folder." "This is ours, readable and running."
8. **Sentence case everywhere.** Headings, buttons, navigation. Title Case Is A Tell.

## Before and after

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Leverage our AI-powered suite to supercharge discovery | Turn signal into a shaped idea |
| A comprehensive framework for product excellence | Thirty-two skills, from the first signal to the measured outcome |
| Seamlessly integrates with your existing workflow | Runs against Jira, Confluence and JPD, which the team already uses |
| Empowering PMs to do their best work | It removes the tax on being a product manager |
| Our innovative solution unlocks new possibilities | Nothing reaches Jira or Confluence unreviewed |

## The four sentences worth memorising

Existing copy, all of it already load-bearing. Reuse rather than rewrite.

> The product process, written down and running.

> Not a diagram of how we intend to work. The thing itself.

> The car does not win the race. But nobody wins it in the wrong car.

> Discipline is the first thing to go when the quarter gets loud.

## Naming

- **Skills** are named as verbs or as the thing they make: `write-prd`, `signal-radar`,
  `jira-epic-groomer`. Lower case, hyphenated, no `oolio-` prefix.
- **The map's stages** are nouns: Signal, Grooming, Definition.
- **The Brain** is the Brain. It is the one internal proper noun this team has earned, and it is
  clearer than "knowledge base".
- **Flightdeck** is one word, capital F.
- Do not invent a second proper noun without a reason. Every one is something a new starter has
  to be taught.

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone |
|---|---|
| Landing page | Confident, specific, argued. It has to be worth reading, not just true |
| Skill descriptions | Flat and functional. They route a request; personality costs accuracy |
| Changelog | What changed and why, past tense, no celebration |
| Error and empty states | Say what happened and what to do. Never apologise twice |
| A deck | Fewer words than feels comfortable. The map carries the argument |

## What the brand never says

- That it replaces a product manager. It removes the tax on being one.
- That anything is automatic where a person signs off. The gates are the product.
- Anything about a customer, a revenue figure, or a roadmap commitment that is not sourced.
  This repository is private and the site it feeds is not.
