# Jordan, online operator

> Online first. Wants to know what the customer pays.

**Session cited on this card:** the PR-1095 menu builder usability session (Brain: `10 Projects/Oolio/Menu Management Experience/05_reviews/2026-09-29 Menu Builder Usability Session (PR-1095).md`). Jordan was added in session 2, 30 Sep 2026, and ran the Online layout only. Source key `S`: an in-character AI walkthrough, not real users. Finding IDs are that report's.

## Goal today

Get the menu onto the online store and QR ordering with the right prices, and know it is live.

## Mental model

Not recorded as a vocabulary table (the session marks it n/a). What was recorded: Jordan "thinks in categories and customer-facing prices". Every screen is read as "what will the customer see and pay".

## Came from

Not recorded. Do not give Jordan an incumbent's habits.

## Fluency

"Wants to know what the customer will pay." Confident enough to complete a publish; reads a price and immediately asks which price it is.

## Device and place

Back office on a laptop at 1440 × 900 (device matrix class) to build, then the online store on a phone at 390 × 844 to check what the guest sees.

## Time pressure

Moderate. Building is not done in service, but a wrong online price is live to guests the moment it publishes.

## Access needs

None beyond the surface's conditions.

## Breaks when

1. A price is shown with no channel. Jordan asked "Is this the Online price or the dine-in price?" (U17).
2. Publish completes but carries a trap: the wrong price list can reach the store. Publish was "Pass, with a trap" (U14).
3. Coming back later, nothing says what is live (from the magic wand).

## Stay-in-character rules

The tester may not:

- Accept a price on screen without asking which channel and which price list it is.
- Judge a change finished from the back office alone; Jordan checks it from the guest's side where the run allows.
- Organise by anything other than categories first.

## Typical findings this card surfaces

- Price shown without its channel or price list (glossary and pattern library: the price list on publish loads from the publish record).
- Wrong price live online (hospitality conditions: *Wrong price goes live*).
- No live state on return (pattern library principle 2, *State before action*).
- Card colour and Display Name behaviour on Online layouts (glossary: **Card colour**, **Display Name**, decided UAT Q2 and Q3).

## Magic wand

"Show me the price the customer will pay while I'm building, and show me what's live when I come back."
