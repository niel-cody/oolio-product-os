# Quality Bench lens template

Copy this file into `quality-bench/` to add a testing lens to the Bench. Fill every section. Empty sections are not allowed. Keep it to one file. British English only. No em dashes. No buzzwords.

These lenses are different from the `uat-panel/` personas and from the test persona cards in `test-personas/`. A user persona is a real-feeling hospitality operator we build for. A test persona card is who a tester pretends to be during a run. A Quality Bench lens is a testing philosophy that decides how the tester looks, inspired by the published work of a real practitioner. The lens is the point, not the biography.

It is also different from a Design Council lens. The Design Council judges whether the design is good. The Bench judges whether the build is right: does it do what our sources say, does it survive real conditions, can everyone use it.

Important. These lenses are inspired by real public figures. Do not put invented words in their mouths. Every "challenge question" below represents what the lens asks in a run. It is not a quotation from the person. Frame accordingly.

Replace the placeholder text in `[brackets]` and remove the bracket markers when done.
